## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**English:**
```
Cedarwings SAS Operations is the internal management platform for Cedarwings employees, supervisors, and managers working on dental aligner manufacturing.

Built for ISO 13485 compliant workflows, the app gives every team member the exact view they need — from the shop floor to the front office.

KEY FEATURES

• Employee clock-in / clock-out with role-based routing
• Production dashboards and daily output tracking
• Quality control and non-conformity logging
• Inventory management with real-time stock levels
• Bill of materials linked to production orders
• Traceability from raw materials to finished aligners
• Machine and maintenance history
• Supplier and purchase requisitions
• Internal audit and ISO compliance tracking
• Customer feedback capture
• Full time reporting for HR and payroll
• French and English interface — switch anytime

ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Each user sees only the pages relevant to their role:
- Manager: full access to all modules
- Quality Control: traceability, non-conformity, ISO compliance
- Inventory Manager: stock, suppliers, requisitions
- Production Supervisor: orders, machines, output
- HR Manager: employees, time reports, profiles
- Employee: clocking and personal profile

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

• Manufacturing data hosted on Supabase (EU region)
• Role-based access enforced end to end
• Audit trail on every critical action
• Designed for ISO 13485 medical device manufacturing

WHO IS THIS APP FOR

This is a private, employee-only application for staff of Cedarwings SAS. A valid Cedarwings account, provided by your administrator, is required to sign in. This app is not intended for use by the general public or by patients.

SUPPORT

For account access, password resets, or technical issues, contact your Cedarwings administrator or email support@cedarwings.com.

© Cedarwings SAS · ISO 13485 · Dental Aligner Manufacturing
```
