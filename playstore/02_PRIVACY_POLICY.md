## 1. Who we are

Cedarwings SAS ("we", "us", "our") is a French company that manufactures dental aligners.
The **Cedarwings SAS Operations** mobile application (the "App") is an internal management
tool used only by our employees, contractors, and authorised personnel.

- **Data controller:** Cedarwings SAS
- **Contact:** privacy@cedarwings.com  *(REPLACE with a real address you monitor)*
- **Postal address:** *(fill in the company's registered address)*

## 2. Who this App is for

The App is provided exclusively to Cedarwings employees to perform their job duties.
It is not intended for use by the general public or by patients.
You cannot use the App without an account issued to you by a Cedarwings administrator.

## 3. What data we process

To provide the App, we process the following personal data about the signed-in user:

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account identifiers | Username, name, role | Authentication, access control |
| Work activity | Clock-in / clock-out times, actions taken in the app, pages viewed | Time reporting, audit trail, HR |
| Device data | Approximate device model and OS version | Diagnostics, security |
| Manufacturing data | Production entries, inventory movements, quality records | Business operations, ISO 13485 records |

We do **not**:
- collect location data,
- access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or SMS,
- use your data for advertising,
- sell your data to any third party,
- share your data with data brokers.

## 4. Where data is stored

Application data is stored on **Supabase** infrastructure (EU region) on our behalf.
Supabase acts as our data processor and is bound by a Data Processing Agreement.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account records: for the duration of your employment plus the legal retention period.
- Clocking and payroll-relevant records: retained per French labour law (5 years).
- Audit and ISO 13485 quality records: retained per applicable regulation, typically 10 years for medical device manufacturing.

## 6. Your rights (GDPR)

As you are an EU-based data subject, you have the right to:
- access your personal data,
- rectify inaccurate data,
- request erasure where applicable,
- restrict or object to processing,
- data portability,
- lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).

To exercise these rights, contact **privacy@cedarwings.com**.

## 7. Security

- All connections use HTTPS/TLS.
- Access is restricted by role.
- Every critical action is logged.
- Passwords are stored per our internal security policy.
- Physical and organisational safeguards are in place at Cedarwings SAS premises.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not made available to anyone under 16.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by e-mail to registered users.
